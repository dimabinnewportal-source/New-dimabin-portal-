ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

An Interdenominational Citadel of Learning

Established upon the divine commission to train godly, competent, and ethical ministers.

OUR MANDATE

A Higher Calling in Theological Instruction

Divine Mandate Bible Institute (DIMABIN) was birthed with a resolute burden: to establish an institution where deep theological rigor and spiritual fervour walk hand-in-hand. Founded as an interdenominational citadel, we open our doors to believers from diverse Christian backgrounds who hunger for sound biblical teaching.

Under our governing motto, "Fear of God without a Mess," our core commitment is to demonstrate that authentic Christian leadership requires both theological competence and pristine moral integrity. We believe that modern ministry demands leaders who are not merely articulate, but deeply sanctified and biblically grounded.

GUIDING COORDINATES

Vision, Mission & Values

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized interdenominational Bible institute, producing spiritually robust, intellectually equipped, and morally sound Christian leaders who will propagate the Gospel of Christ with absolute integrity and power across all nations.

Our Mission

To deliver top-tier biblical and theological education through comprehensive training, mentoring, and practical service, instilling the fear of God as the core foundation for a successful, scandal-free Christian ministry and professional life.
