Admission Process

Our simple, robust steps towards full theological enrollment

1

Application

Complete the admission application form either online or at our physical registrar office with precise detail.

2

Documentation

Submit required credentials, pastoral recommendation letters, passport photographs, and identification papers.

3

Review

Our admissions committee evaluates your scriptural calling, character reports, and academic profiles.

4

Approval

Successful candidates receive their official provisional admission notification via their registered contact.

5

Resumption

Complete standard registration, clear administrative tuition frameworks, and resume theological lectures.
