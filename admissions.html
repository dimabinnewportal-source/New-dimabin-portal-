ADMISSION CRITERIA

Who Can Apply?

DIMABIN welcomes all genuine Christian believers with a verified testimony of faith in Jesus Christ, hunger for theological understanding, and dedication to ethical leadership in accordance with the motto: "Fear of God without a Mess."

1. Spiritual Foundation

A personal confession of Christian faith and active participation in an orthodox local church assembly.

2. Pastoral Recommendation

Endorsement from a pastor, spiritual overseer, or recognized Christian mentor attesting to moral character.

3. Academic Willingness

Sufficient literacy to engage biblical research, lecture modules, systematic readings, and exam assessments.
